# Pitch slides, official template text

The Rise In template has five slides. Design, fonts and colors stay as they are; only the text changes. Each slide keeps the template's own text budget: one title, one sentence, the same number of bullets.

## Slide 1, cover

Big title, two lines: `PARALYX` / `LIRA LINE`

Small line, bottom right: `Lira without selling your XLM · 19-20 September, 2026`

## Slide 2, The Solution

Sentence: Paralyx: Turkish lira liquidity without selling your XLM.

- Post XLM on Blend v2, borrow USDC, cash out lira to your IBAN via a SEP-6 anchor.
- Repay in lira and the collateral comes back. One screen, three actions.
- Non-custodial: Blend lends, the anchor pays out, Paralyx holds nothing.
- Full loop live on testnet; the same contract live on mainnet with real funds.

## Slide 3, PMF

Sentence: Holders in Türkiye who need lira today have one option: sell.

- Stablecoin buying equals 4.3% of GDP, the highest share in the world (Chainalysis 2024).
- Selling loses the position and two spreads; a lira line keeps the XLM, settles in seconds.

## Slide 4, Technical Workflow

Sentence: credit_line, a small Soroban contract, composes Blend v2; the SEPs carry the lira leg.

- One signature: post XLM collateral and borrow USDC on Blend v2 through nested auth.
- Lira: SEP-10, SEP-38, SEP-6 anchor, Reflector rate; USDT0 and USDC repay on mainnet.
